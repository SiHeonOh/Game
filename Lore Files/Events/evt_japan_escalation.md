# Japan's escalation

**Japan's escalation**
- Germany ships materials and know-how to Japan by submarine.
- Japan's nuclear program **fails**, so it turns to **crude bioweapons** (Unit 731), delivered by balloon bombs to the US West Coast, including a **plague attack on San Diego** (based on the real "Cherry Blossoms at Night" plan, which called for submarine-launched planes).
- Somewhat successful: kills people and **terrorizes the US homeland**. America's "London moment."
- Kept limited. ⚠️ **Japan's chemical stockpiles also saved for later.**

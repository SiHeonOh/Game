# Dirty Bombs (Free for All)

- Once the taboo is gone, **smaller nations and factions build crude dirty bombs** using German/Italian program records.
- **Goal isn't mass deaths.** It's making areas **permanently useless without specialized equipment.**
- **Who:** German/Italian splinter states, neutral/minor powers (Sweden, Switzerland, Spain, Yugoslavia, Argentina), civil war factions (e.g., China), whoever has Italy's missing program.
- **Used on:** enemy capitals, ports, water, farmland.
- **Deterrence:** tiny states can threaten big neighbors, so no one can safely conquer anyone. Nations stay broken up.
- **Downside:** crude programs sicken their own workers and contaminate their own towns.
- Result: **smaller dead zones scattered worldwide**, beyond the Black Curtain.
- **[REAL]** Oppenheimer and Fermi discussed radioactive poisoning of German food (1943). US Army researched radiological weapons into the early 1950s. Sweden (1945) and Switzerland (1946) started secret nuclear studies. Argentina's Huemul Project was a fraud.

# Africa

- Almost all colonized in 1946 (only Ethiopia, Liberia, Egypt and South Africa self-governing). When empires collapse, **independence comes all at once**.
- Then the fight over **who leads the newly freed. No one agrees.**
- **Rivals:** ethnic and regional groups (language, religion, old kingdoms), traditional rulers, Western-educated nationalists, **returning WW2 soldiers** (trained and armed), white settler minorities, leftover colonial garrisons.
- **Nuclear tie:** Congo's **Shinkolobwe uranium mine** (Katanga). Whoever holds it has material for nukes and dirty bombs.
- **[REAL]** Borders drawn by Europeans at the 1884–85 Berlin Conference. Belgium's 1930s Rwanda ID cards hardened Hutu/Tutsi divisions. Congo Crisis (1960–65) with Katanga's breakaway; Nigerian Civil War (1967–70). 1945 Pan-African Congress (Nkrumah, Kenyatta). Over a million Africans served in WW2; ex-servicemen sparked the 1948 Accra riots. Kingdoms like Asante and Buganda survived colonial rule.
